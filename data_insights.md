# Data Insights

## Analysis Methodology

The revenue analytics was conducted using SQL queries to extract relevant data from the database. The analysis focused on customer behavior and revenue patterns over a specified period. Key steps included:

1. **Data Retrieval**: SQL queries were used to pull data from the sales and customer tables, filtering by date range and relevant metrics.
2. **Data Cleaning**: The retrieved data was cleaned to remove duplicates and irrelevant entries.
3. **Data Analysis**: Descriptive statistics and cohort analysis were performed to summarize the findings.
4. **Visualization**: Charts and graphs were created to visualize trends and patterns in the data.

## Key Findings

1. **Trend Analysis**: A significant increase in revenue was observed during the holiday season, with a peak in sales in December.
2. **Customer Demographics**: The analysis revealed that customers aged 25-34 accounted for the highest percentage of purchases.
3. **Repeat Customers**: A large portion of revenue came from repeat customers, highlighting the importance of customer retention strategies.

## Insights

- Targeting marketing efforts towards the 25-34 age group could yield higher sales conversions.
- Implementing loyalty programs may help to increase repeat purchases and overall revenue.
- Seasonal promotions should be timed strategically to maximize revenue during peak periods.
